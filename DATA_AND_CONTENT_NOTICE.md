# Data and Content Notice

## German Bundestag data

The application downloads and processes parliamentary material and member biographical data from the German Bundestag. Bundestag data is not covered by the software license of this repository.

- Open Data source: <https://www.bundestag.de/services/opendata>
- Member-data source configured by the application: <https://www.bundestag.de/resource/blob/472878/MdB-Stammdaten.zip>
- DIP terms of use: <https://dip.bundestag.de/documents/nutzungsbedingungen_dip.pdf>

Downstream users are responsible for complying with the current source terms, attribution requirements, personal-data obligations, and any third-party rights. Processed DIP data must retain the required source attribution, and modifications must be identified as such.

The repository does not intentionally redistribute downloaded member XML, parliamentary protocols, photographs, videos, MongoDB exports, or NLP result corpora. Runtime downloads are stored in ignored local directories.

## Photographs and video

Photographs and video may carry rights separate from the underlying parliamentary data. Their discoverability or technical accessibility does not by itself grant redistribution rights. Do not commit downloaded media unless its license and required attribution have been verified and documented.

## Example content

Frontend examples and screenshots must use fictional or properly licensed content. Do not present generated or modified examples as authentic statements by real people.
